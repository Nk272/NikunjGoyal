Receptive field across a stack of conv layers same K & S, repeated

Stacking conv layers makes each deep neuron "see" an ever-larger patch of the original input. For layer ℓ the receptive field grows by RFℓ = RFℓ-1 + (K − 1)·∏ strides. With small kernels this is why deep 3D CNNs can integrate context across many frames/slices.
